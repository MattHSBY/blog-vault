## SSD storage drive(s)
### NVMe (Looking for this mostly because it's much faster)
NVMe is basically a storage drive that you get limited slots for on a motherboard (I have 1 on my current motherboard) but is *significantly* faster than SATA SSD drives. I'd like to use one of these as my main drive and keep my current SSDs as storage drives for large videos and super big applications and stuff, so the drive has to be at least 1TB, but above that would be even better. Here are some options:

### SATA SSD (Still very useful)
I was completely ready to find some good SATA SSD options and put them here, but I'm so surprised because SATA SSDs are currently super expensive, I think because NAND flash chips production is being cut back? I bought a drive a few years ago for £80 (which at the time was like months of saving), it is now selling on amazon (the place I bought it from for £80) at £232.08. Crazy. NVMe is basically the same price per GB as SATA SSD and like 8-24 times faster, so yeah, don't bother with SATAs at the moment, they're just too expensive. At this point, really the best option is NVMe. If I already have one NVMe (I don't as I'm writing this), a second is still a really really good option, but it would require a PCIe to M.2 adapter - which cost probably around £10-15 (that is unless I have a different motherboard from the one I currently have as I'm writing this (MSI- x370 gaming plus), because if I do have another motherboard, I would expect it to have multiple M.2 slots, so I wouldn't need one.)
